# Getting Started with S2N Navigator

This guide walks you through installing S2N Navigator for the first time and getting the application running successfully.

> **Screenshots:** Download the image pack and upload the images to Bolt’s media/assets. Then keep the same filenames and paste this page content into `/docs/getting-started`.

---

## Step 1: Choose Your Access Option

The first step is to obtain S2N Navigator from the website.

Navigate to the **Pricing** page and choose one of the three available options:

- **Free Trial**
- **Launch Special Price**
- **Pro License**

Most users will begin with the **Free Trial**.

---

## Step 2: Get Your License Code and Download Link

After completing your selection, you will see a confirmation popup showing:

- Your **license code**
- A **download link** for S2N Navigator

![License + download popup](/getting_started/getting-started_01.png)

You will also receive an email with the same details.

![Confirmation email](/getting_started/getting-started_02.jpg)

---

## Step 3: Manage Your Subscription (If Applicable)

If you purchased a subscription, you can manage it at any time by clicking **“Manage Subscription”** in the website footer.

![Manage Subscription location](/getting_started/getting-started_03.png)

---

## Step 4: Download the Installer ZIP

Download the ZIP file from the link provided.

![Downloaded ZIP file](/getting_started/getting-started_04.png)

---

## Step 5: Extract the ZIP

1. Double-click the ZIP file — it will open like a folder
2. Click **“Extract All”**

![Extract All button](/getting_started/getting-started_05.png)

---

## Step 6: Choose the Installation Location

A window will pop up asking where you want to extract/install S2N Navigator.

1. Click **“Browse”**

![Browse button](/getting_started/getting-started_06.png)

2. Select the **C: drive** (recommended)

![Choose C drive](/getting_started/getting-started_07.png)

---

## Step 7: Confirm the Folder Exists on C:\

If everything worked as it should, you will see the **S2N Navigator** folder on the **C:** drive.

![S2N Navigator folder on C drive](/getting_started/getting-started_08.png)

---

## Step 8: Open the Folder

Double-click the folder.

![Open the S2N Navigator folder](/getting_started/getting-started_09.png)

---

## Step 9: Launch the Application

Inside the folder, locate the **S2N Navigator** application (the executable) and double-click it.

![S2N Navigator executable](/getting_started/getting-started_10.png)

---

## Step 10: Enter Your License and Activate

You will be prompted to enter your license code. Enter it and activate.

![License entry prompt](/getting_started/getting-started_11.png)

---

## Step 11: Success — Navigator Opens

If you reach the main application screen, the install was successful.

![Navigator running successfully](/getting_started/getting-started_03_B.png)

---

## You’re Ready

At this point:

- S2N Navigator is installed
- Your license is active
- The application is running

Your journey starts here.
